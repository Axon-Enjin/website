# Axon Enjin: Clear signal

Three proposed desktop section mockups, generated with the built-in image generation tool. Open [the review gallery](index.html) or inspect the images directly.

- [Hero](01-hero.png): broad sentence-case statement above one readable stock view.
- [Module explorer](02-modules.png): seven modules in a flat selector with Inventory expanded into a stock-transfer example.
- [Contact](03-contact.png): cobalt closing section, short inquiry form, three offers and secondary Ventures footer link.

## Recommended direction

White canvas, charcoal text, Axon cobalt `#2e6fff`, pale blue selections, thin cool-gray rules. Broad grotesk typography instead of condensed uppercase headings. Simple header, modest corner radii, clear product examples, ample space. Retain the signal as a small blue selection dot rather than a decorative network diagram.

The reference was [GetcKo](https://getcko.vercel.app/). Its public page source, stylesheet and social preview informed the short human copy, light surface and concrete product demonstration. The embedded-browser screenshot connection failed, so this is not a claim of a complete visual or interaction audit. Its green palette, gecko mascot and brand assets are not reused.

This proposes an overhaul of the existing dark/Anton visual direction in `PROMPT.md`; it does not amend that document or replace the running preview. The wordmark rendering in these images is illustrative, not a proposed final logo. Preserve the approved brand asset in implementation.

## Review and implementation notes

The three images form a focused visual test, not a complete homepage. Offers, team, buying information, delivery process and legal destinations still need their own treatment. Preserve all existing navigation destinations, content obligations and accessible controls when implementing.

The generated headings are heavier than intended. Use a medium display weight and check line lengths on a small laptop in implementation. Match palette with actual CSS tokens rather than sampling the rendered images. Retain visible labels, keyboard operation, responsive stacking and reduced-motion support.

Stock quantities and interfaces are illustrative sample data, not customer evidence or verified shipped screens. Inventory is expanded here; an Accounting state must explain management books and export/sync to the customer's registered CAS, without claiming to replace BIR invoicing. Public pricing remains quotation-only. Contact is proposed UI: its submission destination and behavior are not implemented by these images. Custom software defaults to build plus 12 months of maintenance, with the client owning the app and Axon retaining reusable libraries; the compact offer labels do not replace those details.

Content follows `docs/grounding/WEBSITE-CONTRACT.md`, `COMMERCIAL-MODEL-V2.md` and `SITEMAP.md`. Exact prompts and reference roles are recorded in [prompts.json](prompts.json). Hero was generated first; the other two used it as a visual-system reference. No application code was changed in this pass.
