## Proposed transition and morphing behavior

1. Hero to product: Branch panels move together, lose their slight rotation, and merge into the main dashboard. The dashboard scales into the walkthrough position. Limit this sequence to a short scroll distance; keep text readable and scrolling available.
2. Product workflows: Preserve the dashboard shell while the active indicator slides between choices. Animate compatible panel bounds and crossfade the contents between inventory, people, and reports. Clicking must work independently of scrolling.
3. Team: Reveal the photograph with a restrained clipping transition. Keep text stable and legible.
4. Contact: A cobalt accent expands into the contact surface as it enters view; reveal the form after the surface settles. Keep form controls stable during interaction.
5. Mobile and reduced motion: Use short fades or direct state changes in place of large transformations. Keep all information accessible without animation.

These still images show proposed resting states. An interactive prototype is needed to evaluate timing, continuity, scrolling, and actual morphing effects. The existing website has not been redesigned or replaced by this concept work.
