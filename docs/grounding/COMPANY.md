# Axon Enjin — Company presence

For **`axonenjin.com`** only. This host does not sell. Product copy lives on `axlefield.axonenjin.com`.

Plain language: a **5th–7th grader** should follow. Short words. Short sentences.

## What we are

Axon Enjin is a **hybrid**:

1. **Operating company** (primary) — we build and run software for businesses.
2. **Venture studio** (secondary) — we sometimes co-build startups.

We work like **in-house developers**: close to the business, not a distant handoff.

**Company tagline:** *The one who connects.*

**Core project:** **Axlefield** — *An Axon Enjin product.* One card. Visit Site. Not a catalog on this host.

## What we are not (this host)

- A module store, offer ladder, or price page
- A press wall, investor logo wall, or fake portfolio
- A hiring board (until we have real openings)
- The product named Axon Enjin

## About (teach once)

Axon = axis / cable that carries signal. Enjin = engine / the one who connects. We connect people through systems. Axlefield is the product (axle + field). Do not put Ancient Greek or neuron art in the hero.

**Plain scope:** We build software and, when it fits, sit inside a startup as builders. We do not claim tax accreditation. We do not take payments for clients. We do not list work we cannot name.
