Case

Payroll cannot wait on an invoice

Axial · public build · Build on Stellar Philippines · 2nd runner-up

Constraint. Small firms get paid late on invoices. Payroll still lands on a date. Waiting is not a strategy. We did not have a bank as a client. We had a problem and a chain we could actually program.

Decision. Model receivables as something you can split and move on Stellar, with rules in contracts we control. Keep the story small: timing of cash, not a new slogan for the company.

Artifact. Axial is live at axial.axonenjin.com. Four Soroban contracts deployed. Second runner-up at Build on Stellar Philippines. You can click the site. That is the check.

Lesson. This is founder-public work, not a named paying-client case and not proof we should run a franchise network tomorrow. It is proof we can ship a narrow money-timing system and put it on a URL.
